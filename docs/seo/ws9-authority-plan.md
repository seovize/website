# WS9 — Authority / Digital-PR Asset Plan (2026-08-01)

Three link-worthy assets, each built from real Seovize work already done or directly doable — not invented data, not paid placements, not PBNs, not mass guest posts, not automated forum promotion. Each is designed to earn citations by being genuinely useful to someone else's audience, not by asking for a link.

---

### Asset 1 — "90 Days Indexing a New Domain: What Actually Got Indexed and Why"

- **Concept**: A transparent, dated case study of Seovize's own site's indexation — exactly the data this session surfaced. Real GSC screenshots (redacted of anything sensitive), the actual "Crawled — currently not indexed" pattern found in 10 templated city pages, the stale-404 lag on a fixed redirect, the false assumption that repeat indexing-requests fix an authority problem. Published as "here's what we found auditing our own site," with the honest bad parts included (only 8/48 pages indexed after months).
- **Original data required**: none new — this is Seovize's own real GSC data, already gathered this session (`docs/seo/technical-audit.md`, `docs/seo/ws5-indexation-root-cause.md`). Production cost is almost entirely writing/editing.
- **Audience**: SEO practitioners, indie hackers and small-business site owners who've hit the same "why isn't Google indexing my new site" wall — a very commonly searched, high-frustration topic with weak existing answers (most existing content is generic "submit your sitemap" advice, not real diagnosed data).
- **Publishers likely to care**: r/SEO, r/TechSEO, Indie Hackers, Hacker News (a well-documented, honest "we audited ourselves and found X" post is a strong HN/IH pattern when the finding is real and specific, which this is), SEO-focused newsletters (SEO FOMO, Aleyda Solis's newsletter roundups) that curate practitioner case studies.
- **Outreach rationale**: not outreach in the "please link to us" sense — publish it well, submit it where practitioners already look for exactly this problem, and let the specificity (real numbers, real screenshots, real root cause) do the work. This is citation-bait because it answers a real question better than existing generic advice, not because it's optimized to be linkable.
- **Internal commercial page supported**: `/services/seo` and `/services/local-seo` (technical-SEO credibility, "we do our own audits" as founder-led proof) — plus indirectly strengthens the AI-search-visibility case (WS10) since this is exactly the kind of first-party, dated, specific evidence AI answer engines favor over generic advice pages.
- **Production cost**: low — one blog post, uses data already collected, no new tooling.
- **Measurement**: referring domains/backlinks (GSC Links report, already free), branded search lift for "Seovize" post-publication (GSC query data), any inbound traffic to `/services/seo` from the post.

---

### Asset 2 — Texas Small Business Digital Marketing Survey (primary research)

- **Concept**: The existing `/research/texas-digital-marketing-report-2026` page already has real, disclosed methodology (Sprout Social Index, HubSpot, Hootsuite, Pew Research as secondary sources, explicitly labeled "not independently peer-reviewed"). That's honest as-is — the opportunity is upgrading a subset of its Texas-specific claims from secondary-source-plus-professional-judgment to genuine first-party data: a short survey (10–15 questions) of real Texas small-business owners on actual social/SEO habits and spend, run through the founder's existing LinkedIn network, local Texas small-business Facebook groups, and outreach to the industries already covered on the site (restaurants, med spas, real estate, contractors, consultants).
- **Original data required**: yes — this is the one asset that needs new primary data collection, not just synthesis. Realistic target: 50–150 respondents (small but genuinely primary, honestly disclosed sample size — not inflated).
- **Audience**: Texas small-business owners and marketers, local business journalists, chambers of commerce.
- **Publishers likely to care**: Texas-focused business press (Dallas Business Journal, Houston Business Journal, Austin Business Journal, San Antonio Business Journal all regularly cite small local surveys with real numbers), local news outlets doing "small business" segments, Texas chamber-of-commerce newsletters.
- **Outreach rationale**: local/regional business journalists are consistently the easiest legitimate press target for a small, honest, regionally-specific data point — much more attainable than national tech press, and directly relevant to Seovize's actual Texas positioning.
- **Internal commercial page supported**: the research report page itself, plus every Texas city page (this is exactly the differentiation data WS5 identified those pages need to stop reading as templated).
- **Production cost**: medium — survey design, distribution effort, and honest reporting take real time; no paid tools required (a free Google Form or Typeform is sufficient).
- **Measurement**: press mentions/citations (manual tracking + Google Alerts, which is free), referring domains to the research page, city-page indexation improvement over the following months (ties back to WS5).

### Asset 3 — "What We Actually Fixed: A Public Schema/AI-Overview Claim Correction"

- **Concept**: Sprint 2.1 already did real, substantive work correcting overstated claims about Speakable schema, 40–55-word paragraphs, and HowTo schema "causing" AI Overview inclusion — a genuinely common industry myth. Turn that correction work itself into a short, opinionated methodology piece: "The SEO industry keeps claiming schema causes AI citations. Here's what Google's own documentation actually says, and what we changed on our own site." Cites Google's own official statements (already the standard the corrected copy uses) rather than SEO-industry folklore.
- **Original data required**: none new — this is a direct writeup of already-completed, already-cited work (`docs/seo/claim-ledger.md`, the Sprint 2.1 methodology corrections).
- **Audience**: SEO practitioners who've been fed the "structured data = AI citations" claim from lower-quality SEO content and want a grounded correction.
- **Publishers likely to care**: r/SEO, r/TechSEO, Search Engine Roundtable (Barry Schwartz specifically covers "SEO myth debunked with sources" posts), SEO-practitioner Slack/Discord communities.
- **Outreach rationale**: same as Asset 1 — a genuinely useful correction with real citations earns organic pickup in practitioner communities without solicited placement.
- **Internal commercial page supported**: `/services/semantic-seo` directly (this is that page's entire value proposition — "we don't overclaim what schema does").
- **Production cost**: very low — the analysis and corrections already exist; this is a writeup, not new research.
- **Measurement**: same as Asset 1 — referring domains, branded search lift, traffic to `/services/semantic-seo`.

---

## What's deliberately excluded

No paid followed links, no PBN placements, no fake third-party mentions, no automated Reddit/forum posting, no mass guest-post campaigns. All three assets earn attention by being genuinely more specific and honest than existing content on the same topics — the actual differentiator available to a small, real, founder-led studio competing against templated content from larger agencies.
